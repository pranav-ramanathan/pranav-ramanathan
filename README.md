## Research

- **Three methods, one problem: classical and AI approaches to no-three-in-line.** First author. Frontiers in Artificial Intelligence, 9:1799444 (2026). Integer programming is optimal up to 19×19, PatternBoost up to 14×14 and PPO up to 10×10; PPO fails at 11×11. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.3389/frai.2026.1799444) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.11469) · Code: [ILP](https://github.com/pranav-ramanathan/N3L_gurobi), [PatternBoost](https://github.com/pranav-ramanathan/N3L_PatternBoost), [PPO](https://github.com/pranav-ramanathan/N3L_RL)
- **Four Methods, One Problem: The No-Three-in-Line Problem.** Poster at the ICML 2026 AI4Math Workshop (peer-reviewed, non-archival). Adds a gradient-flow solver that reaches the 2n bound up to n = 18. [OpenReview](https://openreview.net/forum?id=svadBHt6YN) · [Code](https://github.com/pranav-ramanathan/N3L_SciML)
